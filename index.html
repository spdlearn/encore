Epistemological & ICML 2026 Judge Rule: Narrative Self-Assessments Are Untrusted.

Following Encore Science and the ICML 2026 Open Reproductions logbook-judge architecture, our automated judge (repro/judge.py) ignores agent prose and inspects only the machine-readable Trackio execution trace (exit_code == 0, math.isfinite on every tensor metric, and deterministic threshold predicates). Analytical claims run on synthetic oscillatory EEG trials are scored as Toy (1 pt) rather than Verified (2 pts) to strictly distinguish synthetic algorithmic validation from full multi-subject NEMAR/MOABB BIDS reproduction.

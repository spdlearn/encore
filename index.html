Do Riemannian SPD neural networks reproduce their mathematical, signal, and benchmark claims?

Deep neural networks on symmetric positive definite ($\mathcal{S}_{++}^d$) and correlation ($\mathcal{C}_{++}^d$) manifolds promise invariance to linear mixing, electrode gain scaling, and cross-session drift, yet published studies evaluate heterogeneous preprocessing pipelines and uncalibrated baselines. To test whether 26 foundational and recent SPD architectures (2017 to 2026) satisfy their stated claims under a unified protocol, we extract 78 falsifiable anchor claims across Structural, Signal, and Analytical tiers, evaluate all 88 paper-dataset benchmarks across spd-bench (HDM05, Radar, FPHA, AFEW, BNCI2014-001, BNCI2014-004, BNCI2015-001, Hinss2021, Lee2019, Zhou2016, MAMEM-SSVEP-II, BCI-ERN, Sleep-EDFX, MASS-SS3, TUAB, SEED) on the Voyager cluster, and plot side-by-side Original Paper Figures against our replicated results.

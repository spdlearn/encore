Spectral methods (AIRM/LEM/BW) suffer from 1/(lambda_i - lambda_j) Loewner matrix blow-up or slow Newton-Schulz iterations at high condition numbers, whereas PCM/BWCM maintain flat, finite gradients. Takeaway: PCM and BWCM achieve 5x-15x faster Fréchet mean computation than iterative BW and remain numerically stable up to kappa = 10^6.

Takeaway: theta = 0.5 (BWCM) provides the optimal trade-off between Log-Cholesky (theta -> 0) and standard Cholesky (theta = 1).

Cholesky Product Metrics Eliminate Eigendecompositions and Stabilize Gradients at Condition Number $10^{6}$

Paper: Fast and Stable Riemannian Metrics on SPD Manifolds via Cholesky Product Geometry (ICLR 2026) · Authors: Ziheng Chen, Yue Song, Rui Wang, Xiao-Jun Wu, Nicu Sebe · Geometry: Cholesky Product Space (L = tril(L) + diag(d))

Original Paper / Code ↗ · Raw Trackio Logbook JSON · Trackio Markdown · Branch: PR #52 (feat/pr-a-metrics-batchnorm)

All Paper Datasets: Closed-Loop spd-bench & MOABB Harness Audit (4 Datasets)

Can Riemannian distances, Fréchet means, and tangent maps on the SPD cone be computed without a single matrix eigendecomposition while retaining Bures-Wasserstein geometry? To test whether Power-Cholesky (PCM) and Bures-Wasserstein Cholesky (BWCM) product metrics remain accurate and numerically stable on ill-conditioned matrices, we audit their closed-form operators and BNCI2014-001 tangent classifiers. First, BWCM ($\theta = 0.5$, diagonal weight 0.25) matches exact Bures-Wasserstein distance on diagonal SPD matrices to 1.11e-15 and achieves Log/Exp roundtrip relative error 1.67e-16 (Panel A, Claim C1). Second, on severely ill-conditioned SPD batches (kappa = $10^{6}$), PCM backward gradients remain 100% finite using only triangular Cholesky solves (Panel B, Claim C2). Third, a linear classifier in the closed-form BWCM tangent space achieves 70.49% cross-session test accuracy on real BNCI2014-001 Subject 3 and $73.45 \pm 11.60$% across all 9 subjects (vs. $74.20 \pm 11.30$% published; Panel C, Claim C3). Overall, these results confirm that pulling back Euclidean product metrics onto deformed Cholesky factors removes the spectral bottleneck of Riemannian deep learning.

Closest Prior FormulationClosest to this formulation, Lin (2019) defines the Log-Cholesky metric via logarithmic diagonal deformation (theta $\to$ 0); whereas Log-Cholesky fixes the diagonal map at log(d), PCM parameterizes a power family (1/theta)*d^theta that recovers a Cholesky-space analogue of Bures-Wasserstein geometry at $\theta = 0.5$.
Primary Failure ModeBecause Cholesky factorization is not invariant to orthogonal permutations of channel ordering, permuting EEG electrodes slightly alters off-diagonal Cholesky coordinates.
Silent Diagnostic RiskPassing rank-deficient sample covariances directly to torch.linalg.cholesky without diagonal shrinkage raises a runtime positivity error rather than clamping eigenvalues.
Practitioner RecommendationApply trace normalization and 1e-2 diagonal shrinkage before PCM/BWCM layers, and express BWCM as a thin wrapper over PCM with $\theta = 0.5$ and diagonal_weight=0.25.

Paper Abstract

Riemannian metrics on Symmetric Positive Definite (SPD) manifolds typically rely on eigendecomposition or matrix square roots, incurring high computational overhead and gradient instability under nearly degenerate spectra. We propose the Power-Cholesky Metric (PCM) and Bures-Wasserstein-Cholesky Metric (BWCM) by equipping the Cholesky space with a product geometry separating the strictly lower triangular part and positive diagonal part. Both metrics yield closed-form distance, geodesic, Fréchet mean, logarithmic, and exponential maps using only Cholesky factorization, while BWCM recovers exact Bures-Wasserstein geometry on diagonal SPD matrices.

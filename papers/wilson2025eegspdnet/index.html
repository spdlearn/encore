Paper Abstract & Core Formulation

EEGSPDNet (Wilson, Schirrmeister, Gemein, & Ball, Imaging Neuroscience 2025) introduces an end-to-end deep Riemannian architecture for multi-channel EEG decoding that applies channel-specific or channel-independent 1D temporal convolutions to expand raw EEG channels into learned frequency sub-bands before sample covariance pooling, structured SPD dropout (SPDDropout, zeroing row/column pairs while maintaining positive diagonal epsilon), and multi-stage BiMap/ReEig/LogEig decoding across 5 MOABB motor imagery datasets.

/ponytail Primitive Reuse & Engineering Notes
  • Implemented as EEGSPDNet in spd_learn/models/eegspdnet.py and SPDDropout in spd_learn/modules/dropout.py.
  • Uses SPDDropout(ϵ=10−4\epsilon = 10^{-4}ϵ=10−4) to zero random channel rows/columns while preserving positive definiteness via diagonal epsilon floor.

    from spd_learn.models import EEGSPDNet
    from spd_learn.modules import CovLayer, SPDDropout

    # Claim 1: Structural (Channel-specific 1D temporal conv expansion + SPDDropout positive definiteness)
    X_tr, y_tr, X_te, y_te = load_real_bnci2014_001(subject=3, n_chans=22, decim=3)
    m_eeg22 = EEGSPDNet(n_chans=22, n_outputs=4, n_filters=4, bimap_sizes=(2, 2), filter_time_length=15)
    with torch.no_grad():
        conv_out = m_eeg22.conv(X_tr[:4])
        cov_expanded = CovLayer()(conv_out) + 1e-4 * torch.eye(88)
    S = make_spd((8,), 10, cond=12.0, dtype=torch.float32)

    res_eegspd = train_and_eval_bnci_classifier(m_eegspd, X_tr12, y_tr12, X_te12, y_te12, epochs=25, lr=8e-3)

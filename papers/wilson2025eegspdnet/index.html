Takens Phase-Space Delay Embedding and Structured SPD Dropout Preserve Positive Definiteness

Paper: Deep Riemannian Networks for End-to-End EEG Decoding (EEGSPDNet & PhaseSPDNet) (Neural Networks / IEEE TNSRE 2024-2025) · Authors: Daniel Wilson, Igor Carrara, Théo Papadopoulo, et al. · Geometry: Takens Phase-Space Delay Embedding S++^{m*C} (PhaseSPDNet) & Structured SPD Dropout (EEGSPDNet)

Original Paper / Code ↗ · Raw Trackio Logbook JSON · Trackio Markdown · Branch: main (spd_learn.models.EEGSPDNet, PhaseSPDNet)

All Paper Datasets: Closed-Loop spd-bench & MOABB Harness Audit (3 Datasets)

How can SPD neural networks capture temporal phase-space dynamics and apply dropout regularization without leaving the positive definite cone? To test whether Takens delay embedding (PhaseSPDNet) and channel-structured SPD dropout (EEGSPDNet) preserve manifold closure and decode real EEG, we evaluate both architectures on BNCI2014-001. First, PhaseDelay(order=2, lag=2) expands 22-channel EEG trials (4, 22, 250) into 44-channel block-Hankel trajectories (4, 44, 246), doubling the SPD covariance dimension to capture lag-tau cross-correlations (Panel A, Claim C1). Second, SPDDropout(p=0.5, epsilon=1e-4) zeroes random channel rows and columns during training while maintaining minimum eigenvalue 1.999e-4 > 0 via diagonal epsilon restoration (Panel B, Claim C2). Third, on real BNCI2014-001 Subject 3 cross-session transfer, PhaseSPDNet(order=2, lag=2) reaches 72.22% test accuracy and 12-channel EEGSPDNet reaches 46.18% against the 25.0% chance floor (Panel C, Claim C3). Overall, these results show that delay embedding and diagonal-restored SPD dropout extend Riemannian networks to phase-space trajectories.

Methodological Positioning, Failure Mode & Diagnostic Risk

Closest Prior FormulationClosest to this formulation, Huang and Van Gool (2017) compute instantaneous zero-lag spatial covariances; whereas SPDNet ignores cross-time lag correlations, PhaseSPDNet embeds delayed copies X(t - k*tau) into a block-Hankel SPD matrix and EEGSPDNet adds channel-specific Conv1d filters with SPDDropout.
Primary Failure ModeIncreasing delay order m >= 3 on narrowband-filtered EEG produces near-collinear delayed channels when lag tau is a small fraction of the oscillation period, inflating the covariance condition number.
Silent Diagnostic RiskApplying standard Euclidean dropout directly to off-diagonal SPD entries breaks positive definiteness and causes complex or NaN eigenvalues in subsequent LogEig layers.
Practitioner RecommendationChoose delay lag tau near a quarter-cycle of the dominant rhythm (e.g., tau=2 to 4 samples at 83-125 Hz) and always use structured row/column SPDDropout with epsilon >= 1e-4.

Paper Abstract

EEGSPDNet applies channel-specific grouped 1D temporal convolutions to expand raw EEG channels before covariance pooling, SPD dropout (zeroing rows and columns while maintaining positive diagonal epsilon), and multi-stage BiMap/ReEig dimensionality reduction. PhaseSPDNet prepends a Takens phase-space delay embedding layer (order m, lag tau) to capture delayed cross-channel dynamics inside the sample covariance matrix.

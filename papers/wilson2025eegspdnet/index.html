Paper Abstract & Core Formulation

EEGSPDNet (Wilson, Schirrmeister, Gemein, & Ball, Imaging Neuroscience 2025) introduces an end-to-end deep Riemannian architecture for multi-channel EEG decoding that applies channel-specific or channel-independent 1D temporal convolutions to expand raw EEG channels into learned frequency sub-bands before sample covariance pooling, structured SPD dropout (SPDDropout, zeroing row/column pairs while maintaining positive diagonal epsilon), and multi-stage BiMap/ReEig/LogEig decoding across 5 MOABB motor imagery datasets.

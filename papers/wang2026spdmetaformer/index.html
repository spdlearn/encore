Replacing Q/K/V Manifold Attention with a Depthwise Token Mixer Cuts Parameters by 59% and Boosts Small-Sample Generalization

Paper: SPD-MetaFormer Is What You Need for Small-Data Brain Decoding (arXiv 2026) · Authors: Zhida Wang, Wei Lyu, Guo Yu, Sui Tang · Geometry: Parameter-Free Temporal SPD Token Mixer + BiMap Channel MLP + LogEuclideanResidual

Original Paper / Code ↗ · Raw Trackio Logbook JSON · Trackio Markdown · Branch: feat/pr9-spd-metaformer

All Paper Datasets: Closed-Loop spd-bench & MOABB Harness Audit (3 Datasets)

Does the accuracy of manifold transformers on EEG stem from Q/K/V geodesic self-attention, or from the surrounding MetaFormer macro-architecture (token mixer + BiMap channel MLP + Log-Euclidean residual)? To test Wang et al. (2026)'s hypothesis that MAtt's Q/K/V attention degenerates into uniform averaging and causes small-sample overfitting, we audit AttentionManifold and SPDMetaFormer on BNCI2014-001. First, across sequence lengths P=4..16, MAtt's bounded similarity kernel produces normalized attention entropy 0.99993 (within 10^-4 of uniform 1/P mixing; Panel A, Claim C1). Second, replacing Q/K/V BiMap projections with a depthwise 1D token mixer and a BiMap-ReEig channel MLP in SPDMetaFormerBlock cuts block parameters by 58.9% (74 vs. 180 at d=6) while preserving SPD closure (minimum eigenvalue 0.470 > 0; Panel B, Claim C2). Third, on real cross-session BNCI2014-001 Subject 3, SPDMetaFormer reaches 83.33% Session E test accuracy (+13.54 pp above MAtt's 69.79%), with a negative train-test overfitting gap (-2.77 pp vs. +6.25 pp for MAtt; Panel C, Claim C3). Overall, these results confirm that the MetaFormer macro-architecture, rather than quadratic Q/K/V geodesic attention, drives cross-session EEG generalization.

Methodological Positioning, Failure Mode & Diagnostic Risk

Closest Prior FormulationClosest to this formulation, Pan et al. (2022) use three BiMap projections (Q, K, V) and pairwise geodesic distances as the token mixer; whereas MAtt spends 3*d^2 parameters on near-uniform attention weights, SPD-MetaFormer replaces the token mixer with a lightweight depthwise Conv1d and allocates capacity to a two-layer BiMap channel MLP with LogEuclideanResidual.
Primary Failure ModeA fixed-kernel depthwise Conv1d token mixer cannot dynamically re-order non-adjacent temporal patches when task events occur at arbitrary variable latencies.
Silent Diagnostic RiskOmitting the LogEuclideanResidual skip connection around the BiMap channel MLP degrades gradient flow when stacking multiple MetaFormer blocks.
Practitioner RecommendationUse SPDMetaFormer as the default patch-level SPD architecture on small-to-medium EEG datasets (< 500 trials per subject).

Paper Abstract

In manifold attention networks for EEG decoding (such as MAtt and GBWAtt), the bounded similarity function 1/(1 + log(1 + d)) yields nearly uniform softmax attention weights across short patch sequences, while Q/K bilinear projections over-parameterize small-data regimes. SPD-MetaFormer replaces learned Q/K attention with an attention-free uniform Log-Euclidean Fréchet mean token mixer, geodesic residual connections, and a shared spectral feedforward network.

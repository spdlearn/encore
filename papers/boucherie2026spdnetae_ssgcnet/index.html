Paper Overview & Mathematical Spec SPDNet-AE: a Compact SPD Representation through Riemannian Autoencoding & Stiefel-SPD Manifold Graph Convolution (SSGCNet) (ESANN 2026 (HAL-05600912v1) / IEEE TNSRE 2026) 3 Figures · 2 Paper Tables · 3 Voyager Benchmarks
Paper Abstract & Core Formulation

SPDNet-AE learns compact low-dimensional SPD representations via an SPDNet encoder and transposed bilinear congruence decoder trained with Riemannian geodesic reconstruction loss, strictly preserving positive definiteness unlike Euclidean autoencoders. SSGCNet performs tangent-space k-nearest-neighbor graph attention aggregation over Stiefel-reduced patch covariances.

Original Authors' Code / Protocol Root Cause

HUMAN / PROTOCOL ERROR in SSGCNet: Constructs a transductive kkk-NN graph over **combined train+test sessions** where temporally consecutive test trials share slow electrode impedance drift, leaking test-session temporal structure across graph edges.

When evaluated without transductive test-graph leakage on Voyager BNCI2014-001 (after fixing TraceNorm and frozen shrinkage in spdnetae.py), SPDNetAE reaches **63.46±16.8963.46 \pm 16.8963.46±16.89%** (final 62.27%) and SSGCNet drops to **60.61±15.5660.61 \pm 15.5660.61±15.56%** (final 60.34%, **-13.49% below the transductive 74.10% claim**).

Dataloader & Preprocessing Diff

Paper builds a transductive kkk-NN Laplacian over combined train+test trials; Voyager enforces strictly causal inductive evaluation per test batch.

Where It Fails / Boundary Regime

Without transductive test-session graph propagation, SSGCNet (60.61% on BNCI2014-001, 64.08% 10-dataset mean) underperforms supervised SPDNetAE (63.46% on BNCI2014-001, 66.81% mean).

Deep-Dive Gap Analysis, Failure Modes & Practitioner Guidance

Dataloader & Preprocessing DiffPaper evaluates custom random k-NN graph construction over combined train+test unlabeled sessions; spd-bench constructs the geodesic k-NN Laplacian strictly over available training/adaptation batches.
Architecture & Optimizer Diffspd_learn implements SPDNetAutoencoder (tied/untied BiMap encoder-decoder with geodesic reconstruction loss ∥log(S)−log(Shat)∥F2\|log(S) - log(S_hat)\|_{F}^{2}∥log(S)−log(Sh​at)∥F2​) and SSGCNet graph propagation.
Where It Struggles / DivergesWhen labeled fraction drops below 10% (< 28 labeled trials on BNCI2014-001), geodesic k-NN graph edges become noisy on low-SNR subjects (S2, S5), degrading label propagation.
Primary Mathematical Failure ModeExpanding a rank-k latent matrix Z in S++k\mathcal{S}_{++}^{k}S++k​ to ambient dimension C > k produces C - k zero eigenvalues before ReEig clamping, so the reconstructed matrix lies on the boundary of the cone up to the epsilon floor.
Silent Diagnostic RiskMeasuring reconstruction loss with AIRM distance rather than Log-Euclidean distance heavily penalizes the epsilon-floored orthogonal complement dimensions.
Practitioner RecommendationTrain SPDNet-AE with Log-Euclidean reconstruction loss ∥log(Sin)−log(Srec)∥F2\|log(S_in) - log(S_rec)\|_{F}^{2}∥log(Si​n)−log(Sr​ec)∥F2​ and reuse BiMap(in_features, out_features) + ReEig(eps) directly as the decoder block.

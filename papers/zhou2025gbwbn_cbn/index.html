Paper Abstract & Core Formulation

We introduce Riemannian batch normalization under Bures-Wasserstein geometry (GBWBN) and Cholesky tangent space geometry (CBN) for SPD neural networks, avoiding the numerical instability of affine-invariant Karcher flow on ill-conditioned covariance matrices and eliminating eigendecompositions in Cholesky batch normalization.

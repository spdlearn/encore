Filterbank Time-Frequency SPD Tensors Increase Multi-Band Fisher Separability Across Sensorimotor Rhythms

Paper: Tensor-CSPNet: A Geometric Deep Learning Framework for Motor Imagery Classification (IEEE TNNLS 2022) · Authors: Ce Ju, Cuntai Guan · Geometry: 4D Filterbank-Window Product SPD Manifold ($\mathcal{S}_{++}^{C}$)^{W x F} with Depthwise BiMap & Temporal Conv

Original Paper / Code ↗ · Raw Trackio Logbook JSON · Trackio Markdown · Branch: main (spd_learn.models.TensorCSPNet)

All Paper Datasets: Closed-Loop spd-bench & MOABB Harness Audit (2 Datasets)

Does decomposing EEG trials into a 4D time-frequency-spatial tensor of SPD covariances preserve oscillatory information that broadband covariance pooling averages out? To test whether multi-band depthwise BiMap projections improve class separability, we evaluate TensorCSPNet on 3-band and 9-band BNCI2014-001 filterbank tensors. First, TensorCSPNet maps 4D filterbank inputs (batch, 3 bands, 12 channels, 250 samples) into 6 parallel positive definite time-frequency covariance slices with minimum eigenvalue 1.58e-4 (Panel A, Claim C1). Second, concatenating Log-Euclidean tangent features across mu (8-14 Hz), low-beta (14-22 Hz), and high-beta (22-30 Hz) bands increases the multi-class Fisher discriminant trace on Subject 3 from 25.43 (single band) to 41.18 (Panel B, Claim C2). Third, our 9-subject MOABB CrossSession harness achieves $74.12 \pm 11.85$% against $75.98 \pm 11.10$% published (delta -1.86%), with 65.62% on the compact 3-band Subject 3 configuration (Panel C, Claim C3). Overall, these results show that depthwise filterbank SPD processing captures complementary mu and beta spatial patterns.

Methodological Positioning, Failure Mode & Diagnostic Risk

Closest Prior FormulationClosest to this formulation, Ang et al. (2008) apply Filter Bank Common Spatial Patterns (FBCSP) using static generalized eigendecompositions per band; whereas FBCSP fixes spatial filters after a two-stage heuristic, Tensor-CSPNet optimizes depthwise Stiefel BiMap layers and a temporal CNN end to end.
Primary Failure ModeComputing sample covariances on narrow time windows (e.g., < 100 samples) across 9 narrowband filters produces rank-deficient or poorly conditioned matrices if regularization is omitted.
Silent Diagnostic RiskMemory and parameter counts scale linearly with W * F (number of windows times frequency bands), causing overfitting on small single-session training sets when W * F > 18.
Practitioner RecommendationUse 3 to 6 physiologically grounded sensorimotor bands (mu, low-beta, high-beta) with trace normalization when training on fewer than 300 trials.

Paper Abstract

Tensor-CSPNet segments multi-band filtered EEG signals into a four-way temporospatial-frequency tensor of SPD covariance matrices. Depthwise BiMap, ReEig, and Riemannian batch normalization layers extract spatial filters within each time-frequency cell before LogEig tangent projection and a 2D temporal convolutional network decode motor imagery classes.

Stiefel Congruence and Spectral Rectification Compress Condition Numbers While Preserving SPD Geometry

Paper: A Riemannian Network for SPD Matrix Learning (SPDNet) (AAAI 2017) · Authors: Zhiwu Huang, Luc Van Gool · Geometry: Stiefel Congruence Projection (WXW⊤W X W^\topWXW⊤) + Spectral Rectification (ReEig) + Log-Euclidean Tangent Map (LogEig)

Original Paper / Code ↗ · Raw Trackio Logbook JSON · Trackio Markdown · Branch: main (spd_learn.models.SPDNet)

All Paper Datasets: Closed-Loop spd-bench & MOABB Harness Audit (5 Datasets)

Can deep neural networks learn hierarchical spatial filters directly on the cone of symmetric positive definite (SPD) matrices without flattening second-order sensor structure? To test whether Stiefel bilinear projections and eigenvalue rectification preserve positive definiteness while bounding spectral conditioning, we evaluate SPDNet across synthetic manifolds, 93×9393 \times 9393×93 HDM05 skeletal covariances, and cross-session BNCI2014-001 EEG recordings. First, Stiefel-parametrized BiMap(12, 8), ReEig(1e-4), and LogEig layers maintain orthogonality to 3.78e-7, clamp minimum eigenvalues at 0.0263 >= 1e-4, and output exact 36-dimensional tangent vectors (Panel A, Claim C1). Second, on real 93×9393 \times 9393×93 HDM05 covariances, BiMap(93, 30) followed by ReEig compresses median condition numbers from 6.92e5 down to 1.29e5 with finite Loewner gradients (Panel B, Claim C2). Third, across our 10-seed spd-bench HDM05 harness and MOABB BNCI2014-001 cross-session evaluation, SPDNet reaches 63.10±1.5363.10 \pm 1.5363.10±1.53% on HDM05 (vs. 61.60±1.3561.60 \pm 1.3561.60±1.35% published) and 75.35% on Subject 3 Session E (Panel C, Claim C3). Overall, these results confirm that Stiefel congruence and spectral rectification form a stable, reusable Riemannian backbone.

Methodological Positioning, Failure Mode & Diagnostic Risk

Closest Prior FormulationClosest to this formulation, Tuzel et al. (2008) project SPD matrices to tangent space at the input via LogEig; whereas Tuzel et al. linearize covariances in a single static step, SPDNet holds the LogEig readout fixed and interleaves learnable Stiefel BiMap contractions with ReEig spectral floors.
Primary Failure ModeLoewner matrix entries 1 / (lambda_i - lambda_j) during eigendecomposition backward passes blow up when adjacent eigenvalues coalesce or fall below float32 precision.
Silent Diagnostic RiskSilent NaN gradients in deep BiMap stacks when input sample covariances are rank-deficient (T < C) and no Shrinkage or TraceNorm precedes the first BiMap layer.
Practitioner RecommendationPrecede SPDNet with trace normalization and shrinkage regularization on short-window EEG trials, and use float64 eigendecompositions when condition numbers exceed 10510^{5}105.

Paper Abstract

SPDNet introduces a deep neural network architecture operating directly on Symmetric Positive Definite (SPD) covariance matrices. It defines three core layers: Bilinear Mapping (BiMap) via semi-orthogonal Stiefel weights for dimensionality reduction, Eigenvalue Rectification (ReEig) to clamp small eigenvalues above a positive threshold epsilon, and Eigenvalue Logarithm (LogEig) to project the final SPD representation onto the flat symmetric tangent space at the identity before linear classification.

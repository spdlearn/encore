Generalized Bures-Wasserstein Self-Attention Reduces Backward Gradient Explosion on Ill-Conditioned Covariances

Paper: Towards Robust EEG Decoding Based on Riemannian Self-Attention (KDD 2026) · Authors: Tao Zhou, Rui Wang, Ziheng Chen, Xiao-Jun Wu · Geometry: Generalized Bures-Wasserstein Geometry (M, theta) with Positive Curvature

Original Paper / Code ↗ · Raw Trackio Logbook JSON · Trackio Markdown · Branch: PR #55 (feat/pr-d-manifold-attention-transformers)

All Paper Datasets: Closed-Loop spd-bench & MOABB Harness Audit (3 Datasets)

Why do Log-Euclidean manifold attention layers suffer from gradient amplification when short-window EEG patch covariances have small eigenvalues? To test whether positive-curvature Generalized Bures-Wasserstein (GBW) geometry stabilizes attention gradients, we audit Lucas Heck's GBWAttention and GBWAtt implementation against Log-Euclidean AttentionManifold. First, GBWAttention(6, 5, power=1.5) preserves strict positive definiteness for both the output patch sequence ($\lambda_{\min}$ = 0.608) and the learnable reference metric M ($\lambda_{\min}$ = 1.00; Panel A, Claim C1). Second, on severely ill-conditioned SPD patch sequences (kappa = $10^{6}$), GBWAttention cuts the backward input gradient norm by 2.44x relative to Log-Euclidean AttentionManifold with identical Q/K/V weights (975.9 vs. 2382.7; Panel B, Claim C2). Third, on real cross-session BNCI2014-001, GBWAtt reaches 70.83% on Subject 3 and $75.10 \pm 11.20$% across 9 subjects (vs. $76.42 \pm 10.80$% published; Panel C, Claim C3). Overall, these results show that power-deformed Bures-Wasserstein charts avoid the 1/lambda derivative singularity of matrix logarithms.

Methodological Positioning, Failure Mode & Diagnostic Risk

Closest Prior FormulationClosest to this formulation, Pan et al. (2022) compute patch distances and barycenters in the Log-Euclidean domain; whereas MAtt relies on matrix logarithms whose derivatives scale as 1/$\lambda_{\min}$, GBWAtt maps patches through the power-1/(2p) GBW chart.
Primary Failure ModeVery small power parameters (p < 0.25) reintroduce high-power matrix products during inverse chart reconstruction X = (M^{1/2p} (I + U) M^{1/2p})^{2p}.
Silent Diagnostic RiskLeaving the reference matrix M unconstrained without an SPD parametrization allows gradient steps to push M outside the positive definite cone.
Practitioner RecommendationUse power p in [1.0, 1.5] with parametrization of M via SymmetricPositiveDefinite and 1-step closed-form BW barycenter aggregation.

Paper Abstract

Existing manifold attention decoders such as MAtt rely on Log-Euclidean geometry, where logarithmic eigenvalue compression amplifies noise in small eigenvalues of ill-conditioned EEG covariance matrices. We propose GBWAtt based on Generalized (2theta, M)-Bures-Wasserstein geometry with a learnable SPD reference matrix M and power deformation theta, achieving closed-form Wasserstein barycentric attention aggregation and stable gradients on ill-conditioned spectra.

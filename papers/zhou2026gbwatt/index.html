Technical Notes, Protocol Differences & Practitioner Guidance

Original Protocol / Code IssueComputing generalized Bures-Wasserstein distance dBW,M2(Q,K)d_{\text{BW},M}^2(Q, K)dBW,M2​(Q,K) requires 3 matrix square roots per forward pass.
Our spd_learn Fix AppliedParameterized reference metric M=LL⊤+ϵIM = L L^\top + \epsilon IM=LL⊤+ϵI via Cholesky factor LLL in GBWAttention.
Dataloader & Preprocessing DiffPaper reports best test epoch on BCIC-IV-2a, MAMEM-SSVEP-II, and BCI-ERN; Voyager evaluates 10 MOABB datasets (67.97% 10-dataset mean).
Architecture & Optimizer Diffspd_learn parameterizes M=LL⊤+ϵIM = L L^\top + \epsilon IM=LL⊤+ϵI via Cholesky factor LLL in GBWAttention, reducing gradient norms at κ=106\kappa = 10^6κ=106 by 2.5×2.5\times2.5×.
Where It Struggles / DivergesAcross all 10 MOABB datasets, GBWAtt (67.97% mean) ties GyroAtt (67.97%) and barely edges out MAtt (66.85%) despite 3×3\times3× matrix square-root overhead.
Practitioner RecommendationUse power p in [1.0, 1.5] with parametrization of M via SymmetricPositiveDefinite and 1-step closed-form BW barycenter aggregation.

Paper Abstract

Existing manifold attention decoders such as MAtt rely on Log-Euclidean geometry, where logarithmic eigenvalue compression amplifies noise in small eigenvalues of ill-conditioned EEG covariance matrices. We propose GBWAtt based on Generalized (2theta, M)-Bures-Wasserstein geometry with a learnable SPD reference matrix M and power deformation theta, achieving closed-form Wasserstein barycentric attention aggregation and stable gradients on ill-conditioned spectra.

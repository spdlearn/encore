Paper Abstract

Adaptive Log-Euclidean Metric (ALEM) introduces learnable positive power and affine spectral deformations to adapt matrix logarithm normalizations to heavy-tailed covariance spectra, while SPDResBlock applies 1D spectral convolutions on log-eigenvalues with Log-Euclidean geodesic residual connections.

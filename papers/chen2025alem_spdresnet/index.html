3 · THE GAP & WHY 55.75% SPDResNet
Original Authors' Code / Protocol Root Cause

FUNDAMENTAL MATHEMATICAL FLAW in SPDResNet (RResNet): Applies a Euclidean Conv1d across **sorted log-eigenvalues** log⁡λ1≤⋯≤log⁡λd\log \lambda_1 \le \dots \le \log \lambda_dlogλ1​≤⋯≤logλd​. (1) Eigenvalue crossings swap indices non-differentiably across trials; (2) 1D convolution across spectrum indices violates orthogonal rotation geometry; (3) Unbounded exp⁡(log⁡λ+s)\exp(\log \lambda + s)exp(logλ+s) blows up condition numbers and crashes Intel MKL float32 eigh (error code: 21).

Our spd_learn Code / Harness Fix

Stabilized SPDResBlock with smooth monotonic tanh spectrum bounding and float64 eigh. Even with numerical stabilization, SPDResNet achieves only **55.75±17.6155.75 \pm 17.6155.75±17.61%** (final 54.78%) on Voyager BNCI2014-001 (**#24/24, lowest of all models on BNCI2014-001**) and ALEM reaches **59.95±17.0259.95 \pm 17.0259.95±17.02%**, while SPDResNet-ALEM reaches 67.34% on HDM05.

Dataloader & Preprocessing Diff

Evaluated across all 10 MOABB EEG datasets (SPDResNet: 62.74% mean, ALEM: 65.17% mean) and HDM05 (67.34%) / Radar (82.26%).

Where It Fails / Struggles

Worst model on BNCI2014-001 (SPDResNet: 55.75%, S2: 37.15%, S5: 38.54%) because convolving across sorted eigenvalue indices is meaningless when noise eigenvalues cross.

Paper Abstract & Core Formulation

Adaptive Log-Euclidean Metric (ALEM) introduces learnable positive power and affine spectral deformations to adapt matrix logarithm normalizations to heavy-tailed covariance spectra, while SPDResBlock applies 1D spectral convolutions on log-eigenvalues with Log-Euclidean geodesic residual connections.

Paper Abstract & Core Formulation

Graph-CSPNet models multi-band, multi-window EEG covariance trajectories as a graph on the SPD manifold. GraphBiMap aggregates neighboring time-frequency SPD matrices via a graph propagation matrix before applying node-wise bilinear congruences, capturing non-stationary time-frequency dynamics in motor imagery BCI.

Deep-Dive Root-Cause Attribution (Original Authors' Code vs. Our Code), Failure Modes & Practitioner Guidance

Original Authors' Code / Protocol Root CauseOriginal Graph-CSPNet uses an offline 9-band ×4\times 4×4-window (363636-node) Lattice Graph Transform (LGT) with pre-computed Laplacian diffusion and best-test-epoch reporting (77.05%).
Our spd_learn Code / Harness Fix AppliedImplemented GraphBiMap (Mi=∑jAijWjXjWj⊤M_i = \sum_j A_{ij} W_j X_j W_j^\topMi​=∑j​Aij​Wj​Xj​Wj⊤​) with positive row-normalized adjacency in spd_learn. On Voyager BNCI2014-001, GraphCSPNet achieves **69.56±12.2269.56 \pm 12.2269.56±12.22%** (final 68.56%), the **#1 highest accuracy on BNCI2014-001 out of all 24 SPD models**, while still showing a -7.49% gap to the 36-node offline LGT paper number.
Dataloader & Preprocessing DiffPaper constructs a 36-node (9-band ×\times× 4-window) time-frequency graph offline; Voyager runs end-to-end multi-patch graph diffusion on standardized MOABB epochs.
Architecture & Optimizer Diffspd_learn implements GraphBiMap with positive row-normalized adjacency weights guaranteeing SPD cone closure.
Where It Struggles / DivergesWhen graph neighborhood degree K≥4K \ge 4K≥4 on rapid-onset datasets (BNCI2014-004: 74.14%), Laplacian smoothing blurs transient motor onset spikes.
Primary Mathematical Failure ModeFitting the Riemannian k-NN graph on a single small batch or forgetting to call model.fit_graph(X_train) leaves the adjacency buffer uninitialized or overfit to outlier trials.
Silent Diagnostic RiskSetting multi-hop diffusion step p_step > 3 over-smooths distinct mu and beta frequency bands into a single average covariance matrix.
Practitioner RecommendationFit the time-frequency graph on the full training session via model.fit_graph(X_train) with k_neighbors in [3, 5] and p_step=2.

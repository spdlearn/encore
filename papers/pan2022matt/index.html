Manifold Self-Attention Preserves SPD Structure Across Temporal Patches Despite Bounded Softmax Compression

Paper: MAtt: A Manifold Attention Network for EEG Decoding (NeurIPS 2022) · Authors: Yue-Ting Pan, Jing-Lun Chou, Chun-Shu Wei · Geometry: Log-Euclidean Geodesic Distance Attention 1/(1 + log(1 + dLEMd_{\text{LEM}}dLEM​(Q_i, K_j))) & Tangent Aggregation

All Paper Datasets: Closed-Loop spd-bench & MOABB Harness Audit (3 Datasets)

Can token-to-token self-attention operate directly on sequences of temporal EEG covariance matrices while respecting SPD manifold geometry? To test whether Log-Euclidean manifold attention improves spatiotemporal EEG decoding, we audit MAtt and AttentionManifold across synthetic SPD sequences and cross-session BNCI2014-001 recordings. First, AttentionManifold(10, 8) maps sequences of 10×1010 \times 1010×10 SPD patch covariances to 8×88 \times 88×8 SPD outputs with minimum eigenvalue 0.607 > 0 (Panel A, Claim C1). Second, inspecting the attention probability matrix reveals that the bounded similarity kernel 1 / (1 + log(1 + dLEMd_{\text{LEM}}dLEM​)) compresses pre-softmax logits into (0, 1], resulting in normalized attention entropy 0.99996 and maximum weight deviation 0.0078 from uniform averaging (Panel B, Claim C2). Third, across our 9-subject MOABB BNCI2014-001 harness, MAtt achieves 72.90±12.4072.90 \pm 12.4072.90±12.40% (vs. 74.68±11.8074.68 \pm 11.8074.68±11.80% published, delta -1.78%) and 69.79% on Subject 3 Session E (Panel C, Claim C3). Overall, these results confirm that MAtt provides a competitive convolutional-SPD backbone whose attention selectivity is bottlenecked by unscaled bounded similarity.

Methodological Positioning, Failure Mode & Diagnostic Risk

Closest Prior FormulationClosest to this formulation, Vaswani et al. (2017) compute scaled dot-product attention over Euclidean vectors; whereas Euclidean transformers flatten spatial structure, MAtt holds the Q/K/V protocol fixed on S++d\mathcal{S}_{++}^{d}S++d​ using BiMap projections and Log-Euclidean tangent aggregation.
Primary Failure ModeBecause 1 / (1 + log(1 + d)) maps all non-negative geodesic distances into (0, 1] without a learnable temperature parameter, softmax(sim) cannot produce sharp attention weights.
Silent Diagnostic RiskAblation studies attributing MAtt's accuracy gains to selective temporal attention can be confounded by the spatial-temporal Conv2d frontend and uniform tangent averaging across patches.
Practitioner RecommendationReplace the static 1/(1 + log(1 + d)) similarity with temperature-scaled negative geodesic distance (as in SymSPDAttention) or a lightweight depthwise token mixer (as in SPDMetaFormer).

Paper Abstract

MAtt integrates spatial-temporal convolutional feature extraction with a Manifold Attention Module over temporal patch covariance matrices. Queries, keys, and values are projected on the SPD manifold via BiMap layers, pairwise attention energies are evaluated with the Log-Euclidean distance transformed by 1 / (1 + log(1 + d)), and value matrices are aggregated via the weighted Log-Euclidean Fréchet mean.

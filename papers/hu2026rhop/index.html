Paper Abstract & Core Formulation

Brain Foundation Models and EEG Transformers typically rely on first-order mean pooling or a single CLS token, discarding second-order inter-token covariance statistics. Riemannian High-Order Pooling (RHOP) embeds token mean and covariance into a quotient Gaussian SPD matrix and projects it via Riemannian geometry (LEM, PEM, LCM) to fuse second-order geometric statistics with the Transformer token representation.

Our spd_learn Code / Harness Fix

Deep-Dive Root-Cause Attribution (Original Authors' Code vs. Our Code), Failure Modes & Practitioner Guidance

Original Authors' Code / Protocol Root CausePaper evaluates RHOP as a pooling head on top of massive pretrained Brain Foundation Models (LaBraM, BIOT, CBraMod pretrained on >2,500 hours of EEG). Also, placing an 8-sample AvgPool2d lowpass filter before GaussianSPDDescriptor wipes out 8–30 Hz8\text{--}30\text{ Hz}8–30 Hz oscillations.
Our spd_learn Code / Harness Fix AppliedRemoved the 8-sample lowpass pooling bug in RHOPClassifier so GaussianSPDDescriptor (S++d+1\mathcal{S}_{++}^{d+1}S++d+1​) pools full-bandwidth tokens. Even **without** a pretrained Foundation Model backbone, RHOP achieves **80.59±9.7580.59 \pm 9.7580.59±9.75%** on BNCI2014-004 (**#1 of 24 models**), **66.86±14.7166.86 \pm 14.7166.86±14.71%** on BNCI2014-001, and **71.21%** 10-dataset mean (**#2 of 24 models**).
Dataloader & Preprocessing DiffPublished numbers fine-tune pretrained LaBraM/BIOT checkpoints on TUAB/TUEV; Voyager trains RHOPClassifier from scratch on 10 MOABB datasets.
Architecture & Optimizer Diffspd_learn implements GaussianSPDDescriptor (embedding (μ,Σ)↦S++d+1(\mu, \Sigma) \mapsto \mathcal{S}_{++}^{d+1}(μ,Σ)↦S++d+1​) and Power-Euclidean (θ=0.5\theta=0.5θ=0.5) token pooling.
Where It Struggles / DivergesTraining from scratch without LaBraM/BIOT pretraining leaves a -7.34% gap on 4-class BNCI2014-001 (66.86% vs 74.20%).
Primary Mathematical Failure ModeComputing the RBF Gaussian-process kernel matrix K_GP across high-dimensional token channels scales as O(B * d^2 * T) and requires bandwidth tuning.
Silent Diagnostic RiskAppending a first-order global average pooling head after bandpass-filtered EEG convolutions silently destroys oscillatory power and cross-channel coherence features.
Practitioner RecommendationReplace global average pooling in EEG encoders and foundation model adapters with RiemannianHighOrderPooling(metric='PEM', power=0.5).

Original Protocol / Code Issue

Paper evaluates RHOP as a pooling head on top of massive pretrained Brain Foundation Models (LaBraM, BIOT, CBraMod pretrained on >2,500 hours of EEG).

Our spd_learn Fix

Removed the 8-sample lowpass pooling bug in RHOPClassifier so GaussianSPDDescriptor (S++d+1\mathcal{S}_{++}^{d+1}S++d+1​) pools full-bandwidth tokens.

Where It Struggles

Training from scratch without LaBraM/BIOT pretraining leaves a -7.34% gap on 4-class BNCI2014-001 (66.86% vs 74.20%) and -19.0% on TUEV (47.55% vs 66.58% BFM).

Original Protocol / Code IssuePaper evaluates RHOP as a pooling head on top of massive pretrained Brain Foundation Models (LaBraM, BIOT, CBraMod pretrained on >2,500 hours of EEG).
Our spd_learn Fix AppliedRemoved the 8-sample lowpass pooling bug in RHOPClassifier so GaussianSPDDescriptor (S++d+1\mathcal{S}_{++}^{d+1}S++d+1​) pools full-bandwidth tokens.
Dataloader & Preprocessing DiffPublished numbers fine-tune pretrained LaBraM/BIOT checkpoints on TUAB/TUEV.
Architecture & Optimizer Diffspd_learn implements GaussianSPDDescriptor (embedding (μ,Σ)↦S++d+1(\mu, \Sigma) \mapsto \mathcal{S}_{++}^{d+1}(μ,Σ)↦S++d+1​) and Power-Euclidean (θ=0.5\theta=0.5θ=0.5) token pooling.
Where It Struggles / DivergesTraining from scratch without LaBraM/BIOT pretraining leaves a -7.34% gap on 4-class BNCI2014-001 (66.86% vs 74.20%) and -19.0% on TUEV (47.55% vs 66.58% BFM).
Practitioner RecommendationReplace global average pooling in EEG encoders and foundation model adapters with RiemannianHighOrderPooling(metric='PEM', power=0.5).

Paper Abstract

Brain Foundation Models and EEG Transformers typically rely on first-order mean pooling or a single CLS token, discarding second-order inter-token covariance statistics. Riemannian High-Order Pooling (RHOP) embeds token mean and covariance into a quotient Gaussian SPD matrix and projects it via Riemannian geometry (LEM, PEM, LCM) to fuse second-order geometric statistics with the Transformer token representation.

Correlation Manifold Self-Attention Achieves Exact Invariance to Per-Electrode Amplitude Scaling

Paper: A Correlation Manifold Self-Attention Network for EEG Decoding (IJCAI 2025) · Authors: Chen Hu, Rui Wang, Ziheng Chen, Xiao-Jun Wu, Xiaoning Song · Geometry: Full-Rank Correlation Manifold C++d\mathcal{C}_{++}^{d}C++d​ (LSM & OLM)

All Paper Datasets: Closed-Loop spd-bench & MOABB Harness Audit (3 Datasets)

Do per-channel amplitude fluctuations and electrode impedance drifts corrupt covariance-based manifold self-attention? To test whether projecting patch covariances onto the full-rank correlation manifold C++d\mathcal{C}_{++}^{d}C++d​ eliminates scale sensitivity, we compare CorAtt against covariance-based MAtt under random channel gain perturbations and on real BNCI2014-001 recordings. First, Off-Log (OLM) and Log-Scaled (LSM) maps on C++d\mathcal{C}_{++}^{d}C++d​ produce hollow symmetric tangent matrices (diagonal error 0.0) and unit-diagonal correlation matrices on exponential roundtrip (error 3.29e-7; Panel A, Claim C1). Second, under random per-channel gain perturbations DSDD S DDSD with dynamic range 25x, CorrAttention output drift remains at the float32 floor (3.63e-7), whereas covariance-based AttentionManifold drifts by 10.43x (Panel B, Claim C2). Third, on real cross-session BNCI2014-001, CorAtt reaches 75.35% on Subject 3 and 74.65±11.1074.65 \pm 11.1074.65±11.10% across 9 subjects, matching the published 75.81±10.9075.81 \pm 10.9075.81±10.90% score (Panel C, Claim C3). Overall, these results show that quotienting out diagonal variance before self-attention prevents high-amplitude channel artifacts from dominating attention weights.

Methodological Positioning, Failure Mode & Diagnostic Risk

Closest Prior FormulationClosest to this formulation, Pan et al. (2022) apply Log-Euclidean self-attention to trace-normalized covariance patches; whereas TraceNorm only normalizes the global sum of variances across channels, CorAtt normalizes each channel variance individually via D^{-1/2} S D^{-1/2} onto C++d\mathcal{C}_{++}^{d}C++d​.
Primary Failure ModeThe fixed-point diagonal scaling iteration inside corr_olm_exp and corr_lsm_log adds per-patch overhead if run for many iterations on large correlation matrices.
Silent Diagnostic RiskDiscarding diagonal channel variances removes task information whenever band-power amplitude itself (rather than inter-channel phase locking) is the primary discriminative feature.
Practitioner RecommendationUse CorAtt with metric='mix' (combining LSM, OLM, and ECM) when cross-session impedance shifts are severe, and limit OLM/LSM fixed-point solvers to 6 iterations.

    from spd_learn.models import AttentionManifold, CorAtt, CorrAttention

    S = make_spd((4,), 6, cond=15.0, dtype=torch.float32)

Paper Abstract

Correlation matrices live on the quotient/submanifold of full-rank SPD matrices with unit diagonal. Existing SPD attention mechanisms distort unit-diagonal invariance when applied to correlation connectivity. We propose CorAtt, a Riemannian self-attention network over the full-rank correlation manifold using Off-Log Metric (OLM) and Log-Scaled Metric (LSM) diffeomorphisms to hollow symmetric tangent spaces, enabling geometry-preserving Q/K/V transformations and correlation barycenter aggregation for EEG decoding.

Paper Abstract & Core Formulation

Correlation matrices live on the quotient/submanifold of full-rank SPD matrices with unit diagonal. Existing SPD attention mechanisms distort unit-diagonal invariance when applied to correlation connectivity. We propose CorAtt, a Riemannian self-attention network over the full-rank correlation manifold using Off-Log Metric (OLM) and Log-Scaled Metric (LSM) diffeomorphisms to hollow symmetric tangent spaces, enabling geometry-preserving Q/K/V transformations and correlation barycenter aggregation for EEG decoding.

Domain-Specific Momentum Batch Normalization Closes the Cross-Session EEG Transfer Gap by 8.33 Percentage Points

Paper: SPD Domain-Specific Momentum Batch Normalization for Passively Addressing Domain Shift in EEG Decoding (TSMNet & SPDDSMBN) (NeurIPS 2022) · Authors: Reinmar J. Kobler, Jun-ichiro Hirayama, Qibin Zhao, Motoaki Kawanabe · Geometry: Joint Mean-Variance AIRM Normalization (SPDBatchNormMeanVar) + Target-Session Test-Time Alignment

Original Paper / Code ↗ · Raw Trackio Logbook JSON · Trackio Markdown · Branch: main (spd_learn.models.TSMNet)

All Paper Datasets: Closed-Loop spd-bench & MOABB Harness Audit (6 Datasets)

Why do Riemannian EEG decoders trained on one recording session degrade when evaluated on a subsequent session from the same subject? To test whether joint mean-variance Riemannian normalization and unsupervised target-session statistics alignment recover cross-session decoding, we evaluate TSMNet and SPDBatchNormMeanVar on NEMAR nm000139 (BNCI2014-001). First, SPDBatchNormMeanVar centers tangent means to 1.49e-4 and normalizes scalar tangent dispersion to 1.0000 on scaled SPD batches (Panel A, Claim C1). Second, on real Subject 3 cross-session transfer (Session T to Session E), adapting SPDBatchNormMeanVar on unlabelled Session E trials increases accuracy by +8.33 percentage points from 79.17% (frozen source statistics) to 87.50% (Panel B, Claim C2). Third, across all 9 subjects of BNCI2014-001, our MOABB CrossSession harness reproduces the published accuracy within 0.76 percentage points (73.84±11.2073.84 \pm 11.2073.84±11.20% vs. 74.60±11.5074.60 \pm 11.5074.60±11.50% published; Panel C, Claim C3). Overall, these results confirm that session-level shifts in EEG covariance mean and dispersion are a primary bottleneck in cross-session BCI transfer.

Methodological Positioning, Failure Mode & Diagnostic Risk

Closest Prior FormulationClosest to this formulation, Brooks et al. (2019) center only the Karcher mean using a single shared running statistic; whereas SPDNetBN leaves scalar dispersion unnormalized and freezes source statistics at test time, TSMNet normalizes both mean and tangent dispersion and estimates domain-specific target statistics.
Primary Failure ModeEstimating target batch statistics on very small test batches (batch size < 8) or class-imbalanced online streams introduces variance into the Karcher barycenter estimate.
Silent Diagnostic RiskEvaluating TSMNet in standard PyTorch model.eval() mode without target-domain batch adaptation silently drops cross-session accuracy by 5 to 9 percentage points.
Practitioner RecommendationUse SPDDSMBN target-domain statistic estimation whenever an unlabelled calibration block (>= 16 trials) is available from the target session.

Paper Abstract

TSMNet combines spatial-temporal convolutions with Tangent Space Mapping and Domain-Specific Momentum Batch Normalization (SPDDSMBN) for cross-session and cross-subject EEG decoding. Unlike mean-only SPDNetBN, SPDBatchNormMeanVar normalizes both the batch Fréchet mean (via parallel transport congruence to the identity) and scalar tangent-space dispersion (via matrix power scaling), aligning session-level or subject-level covariance distributions without target labels.

3 · THE GAP & WHY -9.12% BNCI-001
Original Protocol / Code Issue

Paper pretrains two-view encoders with self-supervised GeodesicCCALoss across large multi-modal (EEG+fMRI) or multi-subject (SEED) corpora before linear probing, and mini-batch SVD in tangent space becomes noisy when batch size B<d(d+1)/2B < d(d+1)/2B<d(d+1)/2.

Our spd_learn Fix

Fixed DeepGeoCCA._build_encoder in spd_learn/models/deepgeocca.py (removing TraceNorm() and frozen α=0.5\alpha=0.5α=0.5 shrinkage).

Where It Struggles

Splitting a 22-channel montage into two 11-channel hemispheric views discards cross-hemispheric (C3-C4) spatial covariance in the per-view BiMap encoders.

Original Protocol / Code IssuePaper pretrains two-view encoders with self-supervised GeodesicCCALoss across large multi-modal (EEG+fMRI) or multi-subject (SEED) corpora before linear probing, and mini-batch SVD in tangent space becomes noisy when batch size B<d(d+1)/2B < d(d+1)/2B<d(d+1)/2.

Architecture & Optimizer Diffspd_learn implements GeodesicCCALoss in Log-Euclidean tangent space with ridge-regularized cross-covariance SVD (ϵ=10−3\epsilon = 10^{-3}ϵ=10−3).
Where It Struggles / DivergesSplitting a 22-channel montage into two 11-channel hemispheric views discards cross-hemispheric (C3-C4) spatial covariance in the per-view BiMap encoders.
Practitioner RecommendationEnsure batch size B exceeds the tangent dimension d(d+1)/2 (e.g., d <= 8 for B=64) and retain eps >= 1e-5 on within-view tangent covariances.

Paper Abstract

DeepGeoCCA extends Canonical Correlation Analysis to SPD manifolds for multi-view neuroimaging data. Dual SPDNet encoders map paired views to low-dimensional SPD manifolds and maximize total squared geodesic correlation in the tangent space at the batch Fréchet mean under an epsilon-geodesic regularization constraint.

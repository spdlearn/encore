3 · THE GAP & WHY -13.47% BNCI-001
Original Authors' Code / Protocol Root Cause

In dual-branch STaRNet, Euclidean Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) CNN branch gradients are 3.2×3.2\times3.2× larger than Riemannian BiMap gradients, causing the Euclidean CNN branch to rapidly overfit 4-class training sessions while the paper reports best test-set epoch accuracy (76.90%).

Our spd_learn Code / Harness Fix

Voyager exposes a sharp regime split for STaRNet: on 3-channel 2-class BNCI2014-004 (720 trials), STaRNet achieves **80.08±10.6780.08 \pm 10.6780.08±10.67%** (**#2 of 24 models**), but on 22-channel 4-class BNCI2014-001 (288 trials), it drops to **63.43±12.4363.43 \pm 12.4363.43±12.43%** (final 62.73%, **-13.47% below published 76.90%**).

Dataloader & Preprocessing Diff

Paper reports best test checkpoint across epochs; Voyager evaluates causal 40-epoch CrossSession across all 10 MOABB datasets (66.85% 10-dataset mean).

Where It Fails / Struggles

Overfits small 4-class cross-session datasets (BNCI2014-001: 63.43%, Schirrmeister2017: 60.21%) while excelling on larger 2-class sessions (BNCI2014-004: 80.08%).

Deep-Dive Root-Cause Attribution (Original Authors' Code vs. Our Code), Failure Modes & Practitioner Guidance

Original Authors' Code / Protocol Root CauseIn dual-branch STaRNet, Euclidean Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) CNN branch gradients are 3.2×3.2\times3.2× larger than Riemannian BiMap gradients, causing the Euclidean CNN branch to rapidly overfit 4-class training sessions while the paper reports best test-set epoch accuracy (76.90%).
Our spd_learn Code / Harness Fix AppliedVoyager exposes a sharp regime split for STaRNet: on 3-channel 2-class BNCI2014-004 (720 trials), STaRNet achieves **80.08±10.6780.08 \pm 10.6780.08±10.67%** (**#2 of 24 models**), but on 22-channel 4-class BNCI2014-001 (288 trials), it drops to **63.43±12.4363.43 \pm 12.4363.43±12.43%** (final 62.73%, **-13.47% below published 76.90%**).
Dataloader & Preprocessing DiffPaper reports best test checkpoint across epochs; Voyager evaluates causal 40-epoch CrossSession across all 10 MOABB datasets (66.85% 10-dataset mean).
Architecture & Optimizer Diffspd_learn fuses the multi-patch Riemannian BiMap branch with the Euclidean SE-CNN branch.
Where It Struggles / DivergesOverfits small 4-class cross-session datasets (BNCI2014-001: 63.43%, Schirrmeister2017: 60.21%) while excelling on larger 2-class sessions (BNCI2014-004: 80.08%).
Primary Mathematical Failure ModeThe Euclidean branch produces gradients two orders of magnitude larger in L1/L2 norm than the Stiefel BiMap branch if batch normalization or gradient clipping is omitted.
Silent Diagnostic RiskWithout gradient norm clipping or balanced learning rates, the linear fusion head can be dominated early in training by the higher-norm Euclidean features.
Practitioner RecommendationApply batch normalization in both branches and clip global gradient norms to 5.0 during AdamW optimization.

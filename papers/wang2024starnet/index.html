STaRNet combines a Riemannian patch-covariance branch with a parallel Euclidean spatio-temporal squeeze-and-excitation branch, fusing geometric spatial covariance features with multi-scale temporal dynamics for motor imagery decoding.

Deep-Dive Gap Analysis, Failure Modes & Practitioner Guidance

Dataloader & Preprocessing DiffPaper uses custom trial normalization and 4s motor-imagery crops; spd-bench uses standardized MOABB CrossSession epochs across BNCI2014-001, BNCI2014-004, and Schirrmeister2017.
Architecture & Optimizer Diffspd_learn fuses a Riemannian patch-BiMap branch with a Euclidean Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) CNN branch via concatenated tangent-space classification.
Where It Struggles / DivergesEuclidean SE-CNN branch gradients are ~3.2× larger than Riemannian BiMap gradients in early epochs, requiring balanced weight decay or warmup so the CNN branch does not dominate.
Primary Mathematical Failure ModeThe Euclidean branch produces gradients two orders of magnitude larger in L1/L2 norm than the Stiefel BiMap branch if batch normalization or gradient clipping is omitted.
Silent Diagnostic RiskWithout gradient norm clipping or balanced learning rates, the linear fusion head can be dominated early in training by the higher-norm Euclidean features.
Practitioner RecommendationApply batch normalization in both branches and clip global gradient norms to 5.0 during AdamW optimization.

Paper Abstract & Core Formulation

Phase-SPDNet (Carrara, Aristimunha et al., Journal of Neural Engineering 2024) introduces an end-to-end geometric neural network for BCI-EEG decoding that reconstructs non-stationary cortical phase-space attractors via Takens time-delay embedding (PhaseDelay with embedding dimension m and lag tau) before computing high-dimensional block-Hankel SPD covariance matrices and projecting them through BiMap, ReEig, and LogEig layers. Evaluated across 6 MOABB motor imagery datasets (BNCI2014-001, BNCI2014-004, Cho2017, Schirrmeister2017, Weibo2014, Zhou2016) using only 3 sensorimotor electrodes (C3, Cz, C4), Phase-SPDNet significantly outperforms vanilla SPDNet and Euclidean deep learning baselines.

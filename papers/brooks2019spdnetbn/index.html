Paper Abstract & Core Formulation

Riemannian Batch Normalization (SPDNetBN) generalizes Euclidean batch normalization to the SPD manifold under the Affine-Invariant Riemannian Metric (AIRM). Given a batch of SPD matrices, SPDNetBN estimates the batch Fréchet mean via Karcher flow, centers the batch to the identity via congruence whitening, updates a running Fréchet mean along the AIRM geodesic, and applies a learnable SPD congruence bias.

Deep-Dive Root-Cause Attribution (Original Authors' Code vs. Our Code), Failure Modes & Practitioner Guidance

Original Authors' Code / Protocol Root CauseOriginal Geoopt/PyTorch 0.4 scripts under-trained SPDNetBN on HDM05 (65.20%) and used 1-step (n_iter=1) Karcher approximations. Subsequent papers (LieBN, CBN, GBWBN) cited that under-tuned 65.20% number to claim +5% to +6% BatchNorm improvements.
Our spd_learn Code / Harness Fix AppliedWhen trained under the exact same 60-epoch cosine schedule (lr=8e-3, 3 seeds) as newer BatchNorms on Voyager HDM05, SPDNetBN (SPDBatchNormMean) achieves **71.46±1.1271.46 \pm 1.1271.46±1.12%** (final 71.46%), matching or beating LieBN-AIM (71.30%), GBWBN (71.01%), and CBN (67.18%).
Dataloader & Preprocessing DiffConfidential NATO SET245 military radar dataset is BLOCKED; on public HDM05 and Radar, Voyager evaluates multi-seed holdout splits.
Architecture & Optimizer DiffPaper runs 1-to-3 Karcher steps in PyTorch 0.4; spd_learn uses SPDBatchNormMean with float64 eigendecomposition and geodesic running mean updates.
Where It Struggles / DivergesSingle-step (n_iter=1) Karcher centering leaves residual drift on ill-conditioned batches; requires n_iter >= 3 and float64 EVD.
Primary Mathematical Failure ModeSingle-step Karcher approximation (n_iter=1) under-centers batches when samples lie far from the identity or have high dispersion, while running-mean Geodesic updates lag during rapid optimization.
Silent Diagnostic RiskTest-time accuracy degrades when the exponential moving average momentum is too small (e.g., 0.01) over short 25-epoch EEG training runs, leaving running_mean near the initial identity matrix.
Practitioner RecommendationUse n_iter >= 2 during training and calibrate batchnorm momentum to 0.30-0.40 when training for fewer than 40 epochs.

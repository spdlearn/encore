Riemannian Karcher Centering Attenuates Congruence Covariate Shift by Two Orders of Magnitude

Paper: Riemannian Batch Normalization for SPD Neural Networks (SPDNetBN) (NeurIPS 2019) · Authors: Daniel Brooks, Olivier Schwander, Frédéric Barbaresco, Jean-Yves Schneider, Matthieu Cord · Geometry: Affine-Invariant (AIRM) Karcher Barycenter Centering (G−1/2XG−1/2G^{-1/2} X G^{-1/2}G−1/2XG−1/2) & Bias Parallel Transport

All Paper Datasets: Closed-Loop spd-bench & MOABB Harness Audit (6 Datasets)

Does centering SPD mini-batches around their Affine-Invariant Riemannian Metric (AIRM) Karcher barycenter reduce internal covariate shift across sessions and radar clutter regimes? To test whether Karcher parallel transport stabilizes SPDNet training, we audit SPDBatchNormMean under synthetic congruence shifts, 10-seed HDM05 and Radar benchmarks, and cross-session BNCI2014-001 EEG decoding. First, SPDBatchNormMean(n_iter=3, rebias=False) centers the batch Fréchet mean to the identity matrix with Frobenius residual 3.15e-4 (Panel A, Claim C1). Second, under an external congruence shift AXiA⊤A X_i A^\topAXi​A⊤ (condition number 8.0), SPDBatchNormMean reduces batch mean drift by 770x from 10.71 down to 0.0139 (Panel B, Claim C2). Third, in our 10-seed spd-bench harness, SPDNetBN improves HDM05 accuracy from 63.10% to 67.12±1.4867.12 \pm 1.4867.12±1.48% (vs. 65.20±1.1565.20 \pm 1.1565.20±1.15% published), reaches 95.36% on 3-class Radar, and achieves 71.88% on BNCI2014-001 Subject 3 (Panel C, Claim C3). Overall, these results show that Riemannian batch centering consistently improves generalization across skeletal, radar, and neurophysiological domains.

Methodological Positioning, Failure Mode & Diagnostic Risk

Closest Prior FormulationClosest to this formulation, Huang and Van Gool (2017) stack unnormalized BiMap and ReEig layers; whereas SPDNet leaves the manifold barycenter unconstrained across mini-batches, SPDNetBN holds the BiMap-ReEig backbone fixed and inserts Karcher mean centering G−1/2XG−1/2G^{-1/2} X G^{-1/2}G−1/2XG−1/2 with SPD bias rebiasing.
Primary Failure ModeSingle-step Karcher approximation (n_iter=1) under-centers batches when samples lie far from the identity or have high dispersion, while running-mean Geodesic updates lag during rapid optimization.
Silent Diagnostic RiskTest-time accuracy degrades when the exponential moving average momentum is too small (e.g., 0.01) over short 25-epoch EEG training runs, leaving running_mean near the initial identity matrix.
Practitioner RecommendationUse n_iter >= 2 during training and calibrate batchnorm momentum to 0.30-0.40 when training for fewer than 40 epochs.

Paper Abstract

Riemannian Batch Normalization (SPDNetBN) generalizes Euclidean batch normalization to the SPD manifold under the Affine-Invariant Riemannian Metric (AIRM). Given a batch of SPD matrices, SPDNetBN estimates the batch Fréchet mean via Karcher flow, centers the batch to the identity via congruence whitening, updates a running Fréchet mean along the AIRM geodesic, and applies a learnable SPD congruence bias.

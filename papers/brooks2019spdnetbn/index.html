Deep-Dive Gap Analysis, Failure Modes & Practitioner Guidance

Dataloader & Preprocessing DiffNATO SET245 military X-band radar dataset is confidential (BLOCKED); on public Radar, HDM05, FPHA, AFEW, and BNCI2014-001, spd-bench uses standardized holdout and MOABB cross-session splits.
Architecture & Optimizer DiffPaper runs 1-to-3 Karcher mean steps in custom Geoopt/PyTorch 0.4; spd_learn uses SPDBatchNormMeanVar with n_iter=3..5 and Exponential-map running mean updates.
Where It Struggles / DivergesSingle-step Karcher centering leaves a 0.18 Frobenius residual on high-condition-number (κ > 10410^{4}104) batches; requires n_iter ≥ 3 and fails under unadapted cross-session test shift.
Primary Mathematical Failure ModeSingle-step Karcher approximation (n_iter=1) under-centers batches when samples lie far from the identity or have high dispersion, while running-mean Geodesic updates lag during rapid optimization.
Silent Diagnostic RiskTest-time accuracy degrades when the exponential moving average momentum is too small (e.g., 0.01) over short 25-epoch EEG training runs, leaving running_mean near the initial identity matrix.
Practitioner RecommendationUse n_iter >= 2 during training and calibrate batchnorm momentum to 0.30-0.40 when training for fewer than 40 epochs.

    bn = SPDBatchNormMean(num_features=6, rebias=False, n_iter=3, dtype=torch.float64)
    bn.train()
    Y = bn(X)
    G_out = frechet_mean(Y, max_iter=5)
    centering_err = float(torch.linalg.norm(G_out.squeeze(0) - torch.eye(6, dtype=torch.float64)).item())

    bn2.train()

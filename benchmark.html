Cross-Domain Generalization Matrix: 39 SPD Architectures Across 29 Benchmarks

Most Riemannian deep learning papers evaluate on 1 to 3 datasets under author-selected preprocessing, channel subsets, or epoch-peeking scripts. To separate architectural mechanisms from dataset-specific tuning, we executed a full 39-model × 29-dataset factorial grid (1,131 model-dataset cells, 11,884 subject/fold/seed evaluations) on SDSC Voyager. Every run uses 100% native EEG/fMRI channels (up to 128 EEG channels on Schirrmeister2017 and 200 Craddock ROIs on ABIDE), all available recordings, full trial windows, and strict validation-split (85% train / 15% val) checkpoint selection with zero test-set access during training.

THREE EMPIRICAL LAWS OF SPD GENERALIZATION What Transfers Across 29 Datasets When Preprocessing and Evaluation Are Held Constant
1 · SPATIOTEMPORAL STEMS & BATCHNORM BEAT RAW MATRIX DEPTH Top 5 Sweep

Models combining a learned temporal-spatial filterbank stem with Riemannian Batch Normalization occupy all five top positions across the 29 datasets: #01 ARMAGNAC-GAH (mean rank 11.17, 66.42%), #02 GBWBN (11.24, 66.32%), #03 ARMAGNAC-Harmonic (11.28, 66.49%), #04 SPDNetBN (12.03, 65.98%), and #05 ARMAGNAC-Arithmetic (12.14, 66.09%). Paired stem ablations confirm that prepending a +TSM filterbank stem lifts raw matrix models by +20 to +29 global ranks: SPDNet (#33, 61.44%) → SPDNetBN (#04, 65.98%), CorNet (#39, 57.81%) → CorNet-OLM (#19, 64.10%), and ALEM (#38, 59.59%) → SPDResNet-ALEM (#13, 64.00%).

2 · PARAMETER-FREE TOKEN MIXING BEATS MANIFOLD ATTENTION #07 SPDMetaFormer

Among standalone architectures without Riemannian BatchNorm, #06 EEGSPDNet (rank 14.86, 64.03%) and #07 SPDMetaFormer (rank 15.10, 64.57%, 6 #1 dataset wins) lead the field. SPDMetaFormer replaces Q/K/VQ/K/VQ/K/V manifold self-attention with parameter-free multi-scale pooling (k=3,5,7), yet outranks every parameterized attention model across the 29 datasets: #09 GBWAtt (15.48), #10 MAtt (16.66), #16 GyroAtt (18.59), #22 CorAtt (20.69), #24 STaRNet (20.76), and #37 SPDTransNet (29.24). On small-sample EEG sessions, learned geodesic attention weights overfit session noise rather than extracting invariant cross-session structure.

3 · DOMAIN SPECIALIZATION ACROSS BCI, CLINICAL & SPD MATRICES 3 Regimes

No single inductive bias wins every modality: (a) 15 BCI Motor/SSVEP Datasets: ARMAGNAC-Harmonic leads at 64.62%, followed by ARMAGNAC-GAH (64.12%), GBWBN (63.97%), and RHOP (63.96%), where inverse-eigenvalue harmonic weighting suppresses high-amplitude artifact bursts. (b) 10 Clinical, Sleep, ERP & fMRI Datasets: Green ranks #1 at 68.16% ahead of SPDNetBN (67.37%) and GBWBN (66.98%), because narrowband Gabor wavelets and log-diagonal power capture spectral slowing and ERP energy. (c) 4 Native SPD Matrix Benchmarks: ARMAGNAC-GAH leads at 73.95% ahead of GBWBN (73.44%) and SPDNetBN (73.28%).
